Jeff Buckley Fan Website
A responsive fan website dedicated to the life, music, and legacy of Jeff Buckley. The site brings together biographical information, music highlights, photography, and interactive elements in a visually engaging experience for both longtime fans and new listeners. 

About the Project
The website was designed to celebrate Jeff Buckley's distinctive voice, musical style, and lasting influence. Its layout takes inspiration from editorial music magazines and archival collections, using a clean structure and atmospheric visuals to create a reflective experience. 
The content is organized into clear section, allowing visitors to explore his background, releases, and photography while discovering more about his music. 

Features
 - Biohraphy: Introduces Jeff Buckley's life, career, and musical journey. 
 - Timeline: Highlights all of Jeff's key life events. 
 - Gallery: Displays a curated collection of photographs. 
 - Music section: Features selected songs and listening notes. 
 - Image viewer: Allows visitors to open gallery images in a larger view. 
 - Category filters: Makes it easier to browse and explore content. 
 - Responsive navigation: Provides a mobile-friendly menu for smaller screens. 

Design
The visual style is minimal and editorial, with a focus on photography, typography, and spacious layouts. A muted colour palette creates a calm, nostalgic atmosphere that complements the subject matter. 
The design adapts to different screen sizes, maintaining readability and usability across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. 

Technologies
 - HTML: Provides the structure and content of the website. 
 - CSS: Handles the visual design, layout, animations, and responsive styling. 
 - JavaScript: Adds interactive functionality, including navigation, filtering, and the image viewer.

Project Goal
The goal of the project is to create an accessible and visually engaging tribute to Jeff Buckley. By combining informative content with interactive features and a carefully considered design, the website aims to introduce visitors to his music while celebrating the legacy he continues to leave behind.
